SOLTECH BRAND DIRECTION
Preserve the existing logo. Its connected dual profiles, faceted geometry, symmetry, and black-and-white contrast should inspire subtle details, not repeated faces, polygon backgrounds, or forced symmetry.

Combine clarity, simplicity, and careful detail with a distinct SolTech identity. Aim for custom and approachable rather than an Apple copy, a generic dashboard, or a recolored template.

Use black, white, charcoal, silver, and cool gray as the starting palette. Explore one signature accent, potentially a refined blue. Choose exact shades through the recommended direction.
